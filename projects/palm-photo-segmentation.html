Palm Photo Segmentation

A comparative palm-image segmentation project that evaluates multiple deep-learning approaches. The work covers UNet, SynDiff, DeepLabv3, and patch-based techniques for isolating palm regions.

Results & Features

  • Segments palm regions from input photos
  • Compares several segmentation architectures
  • Supports preprocessing for biometric analysis
